Better results

The prompt recipe

Claude works best when you brief it like a new colleague. Give it these five things.

  1. RRoleWho should it act as?
  2. CContextWhat does it need to know?
  3. AAudienceWho will read the result?
  4. TToneHow should it sound?
  5. CConstraintsLength, format, and limits.

Copy and paste

Prompt starters

Copy a prompt, paste it into Claude, and replace the red parts with your own details.

Use it well

Four habits to keep

Protect privacy

Leave out student names, grades, and ID numbers. FERPA still applies.

Check the facts

AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Verify dates, sources, and numbers.

Stay the author

Treat every result as a first draft. Edit it until it sounds like you.

Be open about it

Follow CAU guidance and your course policy on AI use.

Meet Dr. Dixon

Dr. Dewayne A. Dixon

Assistant Professor, Department of Mathematical Sciences

Dr. Dixon holds a PhD in Mathematics from Howard University and a BS from Morehouse College. He gives talks on how to use generative AI and on the mathematics behind how AI models are built. Through the AUC Data Science Initiative, he has trained faculty at HBCUs across the country. His research uses machine learning to study complex biological data, such as DNA markers of PTSD.
